The Debian Operating System

Debian is a complete free operating system

Download Try Debian live before installing
  • Why Debian?

    Debian is a stable, secure, and free operating system, supported by a global community and offering thousands of software packages.

  • User support

    Find help through the Debian community, documentation, mailing lists, and support channels.

  • Security updates

    The Debian Security Team provides updates for stable releases to help keep your system protected.

  • How do I install Debian?

    Choose an installation image, create a bootable USB drive or disc, and follow the installer instructions.

Community

Debian is a community of people

  • Who we are and what we do

    The Debian Project is a community of volunteers who build and maintain the Debian operating system.

  • Our philosophy

    Debian is committed to free software, openness, and a stable system built through collaborative development.

  • How can I get involved?

    Contribute by reporting bugs, improving documentation, translating, testing, or packaging software.

  • Where can I get help?

    Start with the official documentation, community forums, mailing lists, or Debian support channels.
